MeTube Ember: the mark

A window whose title bar glows. The bar is an ember, the play sign sits inside, and film perforations run down both sides, as on the Lite and Super icons. It is Ember's own title bar, enlarged.

نافذة يتوهج سقفها: شريط العنوان جمرة، والتشغيل في الداخل، وثقوب الفيلم على الجانبين كما في أيقونتي Lite وSuper.

Colours · الألوان

Espresso#2B211Bthe tile; the name on light ground
Cream#FBF6EEthe window and play sign; the name on dark
Ember#C25E2Ethe title bar only, in every theme

The ember does not follow the light or dark theme: it is the same in both, and nothing else in the mark is ember.

The mark · الرمز

With the name · مع الاسم

The name is Tajawal Bold, drawn as outlines: it needs no font installed. Nothing else is set beside the mark in its place.

Space and size · المسافة والمقاس

Clear space: keep free around the tile at least the height of its title bar (an eighth of the tile). Between the mark and the name the lockups keep their own gap; do not narrow it.

VersionSmallest
The tile16 px, in its small cut below 24 px
One colour mark16 px (the tray), small cut below 24 px
Horizontal lockup120 px wide
Stacked lockup72 px wide

Do not · لا

  • recolour the title bar, or let it follow the theme;
  • put the tile on espresso or a dark taskbar without its light edge;
  • drop the perforations above 24 px, or keep them below it;
  • stretch, rotate, outline, or add shadows, gradients or glow;
  • set the name in another typeface, or retype it as live text.
